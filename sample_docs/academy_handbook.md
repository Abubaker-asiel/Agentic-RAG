# Academy Handbook (sample data)

## Enrollment
Students can enroll in any course up to 7 days after the start date. Enrollment is confirmed once the first
installment is paid. Group enrollment of 3 or more students from the same company receives a 15% discount on the
listed course price.

## Payment and refunds
Course fees can be paid in full or in two equal installments, the second due at the midpoint of the course.
Refunds are available in full within the first 7 days. Between day 8 and day 14, 50% of the fee is refunded.
After day 14, no refund is given, but the seat can be transferred to another person for a 200 EGP administrative fee.

## Certificates
A certificate of completion is issued to students who attend at least 80% of the sessions and score 70% or higher
on the final project. Students who miss the final project can retake it once, within 30 days, at no extra cost.
Distinction certificates require a final project score of 90% or higher.

## Learning paths
The AI path recommends this order: Python for Data Analysis, Machine Learning Foundations, Deep Learning with
PyTorch, then either NLP and Transformers or RAG and LLM Applications. The Data path recommends: Python for Data
Analysis, SQL and Databases, then Power BI Dashboards. Students finishing a full path receive a 10% discount on the
next course they enroll in.

## Support
Office hours run Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 18:00 Cairo time. Technical support tickets are answered within
24 hours. Recorded sessions are available for 90 days after the course ends.
